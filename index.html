OUR STORY

About PROLOGUE

PROLOGUE is the reading club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women, created for students who believe that books can inspire ideas, conversations and creativity.

Our Vision

To create a vibrant community where students can discover stories, exchange ideas and develop a lasting love for reading.

Our Purpose

To bring students together through books, discussions, literature and creative expression.
